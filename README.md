## About me

I'm Andrea, a data scientist with a background in **Statistics and Data Science at the University of Bologna** and an international double-degree experience at **ENSAI, France**.

I like working on problems that require both careful reasoning and practical implementation. For me, a good analysis is not just about finding a model that performs well: it's also about understanding the assumptions, interpreting the results, and communicating them clearly.

My interests include statistical modelling, machine learning, deep learning, and data analysis. I'm particularly interested in opportunities where I can apply what I've learned, work with others, and keep growing technically.

## Research experience

During my internship at **CREST–ENSAI**, supervised by **Valentin Patilea**, I worked on the estimation of mean and covariance structures for **sparse functional data**.

The project combined statistical methodology, implementations in R, simulation studies, and applications to real datasets. It taught me the value of being rigorous about both the mathematics behind a method and the way it behaves in practice.
